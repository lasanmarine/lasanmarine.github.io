Ship design is the discipline at the centre of our work

Our experience spans newbuild and conversion design, specialist calculations and technical documentation across multiple vessel types and hull materials. The same hands-on work also created the questions that led us into R&D and the workflow problems that led us into software.

How our capabilities grew

R&D and software grew from problems worth solving

Some design questions need simulation, measurement or testing before the answer is clear. Some recurring work is better handled by software than by another spreadsheet or manual process. We developed both capabilities from those practical needs, and they now stand alongside ship design as dedicated areas of work.

How we work

Clear scope before the work starts

We agree what we will do, what will be delivered and what information is still needed. That gives both sides the same understanding of the project from the start.

Calculations you can follow

Assumptions, inputs and results are presented clearly so they can be reviewed, compared and used in the next technical decision.

Designs grounded in real conditions

We consider the vessel as it will be built and operated, not only as a set of drawings. Existing conditions, fabrication constraints and operating limits are part of the design discussion.
