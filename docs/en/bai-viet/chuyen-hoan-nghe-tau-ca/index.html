Key considerations when converting a fishing vessel

Technical note · 12.06.2026

A new fishing method changes deck arrangement, equipment, and loading conditions.

Review the general arrangement

Equipment for the new operation must follow a practical workflow while preserving safe access and working areas.

Check loading and stability

Equipment, fishing gear, and catch alter the vessel's center of gravity. Loading conditions must be recalculated for stability.

Update technical documents

Arrangement, structural reinforcement, related systems, and the stability booklet must be updated consistently before classification review.
