Digital solutions and management software

Software built from real work

It started with the data, documents and workflows of ship design. Today we build management systems, websites, automation and AI applications for a wide range of operational needs.

Workflows and data

From in-house digitisation to a dedicated software service

Our first tools tackled repetitive calculations, lookup tables, documents and data. We then went on to build management systems, websites and automation workflows. Calculation tools are still maintained, but they are now a small part of our software work.

Software starts with a specific problem

Some projects begin with a management process, some with a website, others with automation or AI on existing data. Either way, we start from how the work actually happens, not from a ready-made feature package.

Data and work are spread across spreadsheets, forms and separate files

Information is hard to track, several people handle it without a single source of truth, and reports take time to compile.

Our approach

We build a management system around your actual data, roles and workflow, then refine it as the process changes.

Discuss your system needs

Your website doesn't clearly present your services, products or capabilities

There is plenty of content but the structure is hard to follow, updates depend on developers, or the site is weak on search and analytics.

Our approach

We build the content structure, interface and CMS so the site is clear to visitors and easy for your team to run long term.

Discuss your website

Some steps involve too much re-entry or manual handling

Data moves between forms, spreadsheets, email and multiple systems, slowing work down and introducing errors.

Our approach

Rule-based steps can be automated to transfer data, generate reports, send notifications or trigger the next step.

Discuss an automation task

You want to use AI but haven't identified the right task

The data and documents exist, but it's unclear where AI fits and how its output should be checked.

Our approach

We assess specific tasks such as extraction, classification, search, Q&A or recognition. If a conventional solution works well, AI isn't needed.
